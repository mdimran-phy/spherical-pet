# Beginner glossary

**primary** — Particle injected by the configured source.

**secondary** — Particle created by an interaction or emission process.

**run** — A group of events; this application supports one BeamOn per invocation.

**event** — One configured primary and its descendants in the current baseline.

**track** — The history of one particle.

**step** — A segment of a track ending at a process or navigation limit.

**trajectory** — A stored path used for visualization.

**hit** — Here, a recorded accepted optical photon at a SiPM.

**scintillator** — Material that emits light in response to deposited energy.

**GAGG** — Gadolinium aluminum gallium garnet; the present crystal host.

**coupling** — Optical contact material between parts.

**light guide** — Transparent shaped part that transports and distributes light.

**SiPM** — Silicon photomultiplier: many avalanche microcells with a combined sensor output.

**PDE** — Photon detection efficiency; do not reapply it to already accepted hits.

**spectrum** — Distribution of emitted intensity over energy or wavelength.

**refractive index** — Property that affects optical propagation and boundary refraction/reflection.

**absorption length** — Characteristic distance for exponential photon survival in bulk material.

**Monte Carlo** — Simulation based on probabilistic sampling under specified physical rules.

**seed** — Initialization of a pseudorandom sequence.

**Compton** — Gamma scatter transferring energy to an electron.

**photoelectric** — Absorption of an incident gamma, releasing an electron and possible atomic-relaxation products.

**Rayleigh** — Coherent gamma scattering with essentially unchanged energy in this model.

**Cherenkov** — Light emitted by a charged particle moving faster than the phase speed of light in a medium.

**validation** — Comparison with suitable trusted measurements or benchmarks.

**CNN** — Convolutional neural network; no network is trained by the present application.

**coincidence** — Detections compatible under a specified timing and event-selection rule.

**line of response** — Line connecting estimated paired detector interaction positions.
